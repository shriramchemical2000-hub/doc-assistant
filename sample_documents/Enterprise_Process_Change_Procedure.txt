ENTERPRISE PROCESS CHANGE PROCEDURE
Version: 1.0
Status: Approved (fictional demo document)
Document owner: Enterprise Process Governance
Effective date: 1 February 2026

1. Purpose
Describe how business process changes are requested, assessed, approved, communicated and recorded.

2. Scope
Applies to changes to documented business processes, supporting systems, responsibilities and operational controls across all business functions.

3. Roles
Requestor: Describes the proposed change and expected outcome.
Process Owner: Assesses process impact and confirms process accuracy.
Change Reviewer: Reviews dependencies, risks and affected stakeholders.
Document Coordinator: Updates the controlled document register.
Approver: Authorises the change.

4. Procedure
Step 1 — The Requestor records the reason, expected benefit, affected documents and proposed implementation date.
Step 2 — The Process Owner reviews the request and identifies affected teams, systems, records and process hand-offs.
Step 3 — The Change Reviewer checks dependencies and potential impacts.
Step 4 — The Process Owner may approve and publish a process change once the Change Reviewer has completed the review.
Step 5 — The Document Coordinator updates the document version and register.
Step 6 — The Requestor communicates the change to affected stakeholders.

5. Records
Retain the change request, impact review, approval evidence, updated document and communication record.

6. Exceptions
If the change cannot be completed as planned, the Process Owner records the reason and agrees the next action with affected stakeholders.

7. Review
Review this procedure every 12 months or after a material process change.
