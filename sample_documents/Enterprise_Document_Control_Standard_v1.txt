ENTERPRISE DOCUMENT CONTROL STANDARD
Version: 1.0
Status: Approved (fictional demo document)
Document owner: Enterprise Process Governance
Effective date: 1 January 2026
Review cycle: 12 months

1. Purpose
This standard defines a consistent approach for creating, reviewing, approving, publishing and maintaining controlled business documents across the organisation.

2. Scope
Applies to policies, procedures, standards, guidelines, forms and controlled reference documents used by business functions. It does not replace specialist legal, safety, security or regulatory requirements.

3. Roles
Document Owner: Maintains accuracy, coordinates review and proposes updates.
Process Owner: Confirms that process steps and responsibilities are accurate.
Reviewer: Checks clarity, consistency and impacts on connected processes.
Approver: Authorises publication according to the approval rules below.
Document Coordinator: Maintains the central register and published copy.

4. Approval
A controlled document may be published after approval by its Document Owner and Process Owner. The Document Coordinator verifies that the version, owner, approval date and review date are recorded before publication.

5. Versioning and review
Use a major version for material changes to responsibilities, controls or process outcomes. Use a minor version for editorial or clarification changes. Review every controlled document at least once every 12 months.

6. Records
Retain the approved source file, review comments, approval evidence and publication date in the designated document repository. The retention period is determined by the applicable records schedule.

7. Exceptions
Urgent changes may be issued through an expedited review, but the Document Owner must record the reason and obtain the required approvals before publication.
