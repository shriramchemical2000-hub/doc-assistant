SAMPLE EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

This fictional handbook is provided only to demonstrate DocuMind. It does not describe any real employer or policy.

ANNUAL LEAVE
Eligible full-time employees receive 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave should be requested through the employee portal and approved by the employee's manager before the planned absence. Unused leave carry-over is limited to 5 working days and must be used by 31 March of the following year.

SICK LEAVE
Employees may take up to 10 working days of paid sick leave per calendar year. An absence of more than 2 consecutive working days may require a medical certificate under this fictional policy.

WORKING HOURS
The standard working week is 40 hours, normally scheduled Monday through Friday. Team-specific schedules may vary with manager approval.

EXPENSE CLAIMS
Business expenses should be submitted within 30 calendar days of the transaction with an itemized receipt. Claims are reviewed by the employee's manager and the finance team.
